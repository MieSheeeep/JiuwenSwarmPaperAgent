# Baseline scope and future work

This is a baseline and engineering contribution, not a claim of a novel context-management algorithm. Project-ID lexical retrieval is deliberately simple and favored by the synthetic task structure. The useful contribution is a reusable, opt-in budget selector and rail with measured token accounting, exact answer scoring, and an evidence-bound writing pipeline.

Future research should add paraphrased queries, ambiguous references, larger and varied budgets, multiple models and runs, held-out tasks, real tool interactions, stronger retrieval baselines, and checks for summary information loss. Those experiments were not performed in this pilot.
